Our Story

Not just a café.
A feeling.

Tucked away near Yes Bank in Banashankari 3rd Stage, Cafe Eleven isn't just a place to grab coffee — it's where Bangalore nights come alive. Outdoor seating, the soft glow of warm lights, a hookah cloud drifting through conversations — this is where time slows and stories grow.

Born from the idea that every city needs a corner that feels like home, Cafe Eleven is that corner for Banashankari.
